Gurudwara Sri Guru Singh Sabha Ji, Dimapur - Nagaland

The Gurudwara serves as the central Sikh place of worship in the region and is managed by a local Gurudwara Management Committee

On 15 November 2024, the Governor of Nagaland, La Genesan, attended the 555th Guru Nanak Dev Ji’s birth anniversary celebrations held at the Gurudwara. He lauded the Sikh community for its spirit of service, unity, and cultural contribution in the state. He praised the Gurudwara for being more than a worship space—a center for harmony and seva for all communities

In July 2022, Governor Prof. Jagdish Mukhi visited the Gurudwara and praised the Sikh community’s efforts in philanthropy, social justice, and civic well‑being. He encouraged continued service and assured readiness to address community concerns, highlighting the Gurudwara’s role in promoting welfare beyond the congregation
