Gurudwara Dukh Nivaran Darbar Ji
Udaipur  ·  Rajasthan

Situated in the locality of Sec 14, this Gurudwara is quite famous for its calm and soothing atmosphere. Many people also practice meditation in the peaceful climate of this place.

The Gurudwara can easily gather a group of 350 to 400 disciples at a time

Address
Sector 14, opposite Suhalka Bhawan, Udaipur
