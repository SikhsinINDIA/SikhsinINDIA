Gurudwara Sis Ganj Sahib Ji - Delhi

Martyrdom of Guru Tegh Bahadur: On 11 November 1675, under the orders of Mughal Emperor Aurangzeb, Guru Tegh Bahadur was publicly beheaded at this site for resisting forced conversion and defending religious freedom for Kashmiri Pandits

Preserved Relics: Inside the shrine are the trunk of the tree beneath which the execution took place and the well from which Guru Ji bathed during his imprisonment. Beside the Gurdwara is the original Mughal-era Kotwali (police station) where he and his disciples were detained and tortured
