Gurudwara Singh Sabha Ji, Vasco da Gama - Goa

Also known simply as Gurudwara Singh Sabha Vasco da Gama, it’s one of only three gurudwaras in Goa, and the principal one serving in Vasco da Gama.

Offers a calm and spiritual pause from the bustle of South Goa, especially for Sikh pilgrims and travellers seeking cultural insight

Known for its well-kept premises, regular devotional activities, and warm welcome to guests of all backgrounds

Address: Mangor Hill, Vasco da Gama, Goa – 403802
