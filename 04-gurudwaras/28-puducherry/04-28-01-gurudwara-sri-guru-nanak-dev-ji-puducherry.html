Gurudwara Sri Guru Nanak Dev Ji - Puducherry

The gurudwara comprises a modest entrance gate and boundary wall, blending with the surrounding residential setting.

Inside, the darbar hall is simple and functional—decorated thoughtfully for scriptures, with seating on the floor and a canopy over the Guru Granth Sahib

The atmosphere appears serene and community-oriented, suited for regular worship and local gatherings

Address: Verampattinam Road, Puranankuppam, Street, behind Bharathiar Palkalaikoodam, Ariyankuppam, Puducherry 605007
