Yamunanagar  ·  Haryana

Gurdwara Sri Janam Asthaan Pir Budhu Shah Ji stands at Sadhora in the Yamunanagar district of Haryana.

The shrines of the Yamunanagar district preserve the memory of the Gurus’ journeys through Haryana, and of the sangats that grew up around the places where they stopped.

The detailed history of the shrine is recorded on the information board maintained at the Gurdwara.
