Itanagar & Naharlagun
Capital Region
Gurudwara Singh Sabha Ji
Naharlagun, Itanagar
Location: Naharlagun, Itanagar, Arunachal Pradesh

Founded in 1974 by local Sikh families and defence personnel stationed in the region, it grew from a small prayer centre into a fully constructed Gurdwara Sahib through community contributions and sewa.
