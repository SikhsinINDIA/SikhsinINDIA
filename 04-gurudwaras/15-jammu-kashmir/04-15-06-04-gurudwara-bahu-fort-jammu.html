Gurudwara Bahu Fort Ji - Jammu

The Gurdwara was established in memory of Mahant Bachiter Singh, who led Dera Nangali Sahib in Poonch

Though the nearby fort dates back over 3,000 years, the Gurdwara itself is a recent addition, added to honor Mahant Bachiter Singh’s devotion and service

It serves as a spiritual center for Sikh devotees and acknowledges the Sikh community’s presence in the region historically

The structure is modest in size—common for memorial or community Gurdwaras—without grand domes or extensive courtyards

•Situated close to the ancient stone fort with octagonal turrets, the Gurdwara’s simplicity contrasts with the grandeur of Bahu Fort and gardens surrounding it

Address: Near Bahu Fort, about 1 km from Jammu Railway Station, Jammu
