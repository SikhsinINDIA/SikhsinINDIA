Gurudwara Mokokchung - Nagaland

It is recognized as the second‑oldest Sikh Gurudwara in Nagaland, established in the early 1960s under the Nagaland Sikh Gurudwara Management Committee (NSGMC), after Dimapur

Part of a network of four official Sikh shrines in Nagaland (hosts in Dimapur, Mokokchung, Kohima, Tuensang), coordinated by the NSGMC

Supported local Sikh community in Mokokchung since the 1960s, serving as their religious and cultural hub.

While detailed current operational details of the Gurudwara itself are scarce, Mokokchung as a town is the cultural and intellectual capital of the Ao Naga tribe, with a deeply rooted culture, historical villages, and strong communal institutions
* Mokokchung is a major urban centre blending tradition with education and civic life
* The Gurudwara likely serves the growing Sikh and immigrant business community practicing Sikhism, Hinduism, and Islam amidst a predominantly Christian population
