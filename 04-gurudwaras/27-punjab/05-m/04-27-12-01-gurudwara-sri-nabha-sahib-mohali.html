Gurudwara Sri Nabha Sahib Ji - Mohali

The site marks the place where Bhai Jaita Ji paused while carrying the severed head of Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji to Anandpur Sahib after his martyrdom in 1675. An elderly Muslim saint, Faqir Dargahi Shah, provided shelter here and urged devotees to pay their respects at this location

Guru Gobind Singh Ji visited in 1698 after the Battle of Bhangani to honor his father and meet Faqir Shah, who passed away soon after. His samadhi is preserved near the shrine and is revered by devotees

In 1709, Baba Banda Singh Bahadur also visited to pay tribute before launching his campaign against Sirhind
