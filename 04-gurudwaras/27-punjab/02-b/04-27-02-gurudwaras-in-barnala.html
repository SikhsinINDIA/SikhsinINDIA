GURUDWARAS IN BARNALA
Punjab
Faith  ·  Seva  ·  Sangat  ·  Heritage

Barnala district holds eighteen historical Gurudwaras, marking the journeys of Sri Guru Hargobind Sahib Ji, Sri Guru Har Rai Sahib Ji, Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji and Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji through the villages of Malwa.

Gurdwara Sri Arisar Sahib
Dhaula  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji

Gurdwara Sri Arisar Sahib stands between the villages of Dhaula and Handiaya, marking a halt on Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji’s journey through Malwa.

Gurdwara Sri Gurusar Sahib Patshahi Nauvin
Kattu  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji

At Kattu, Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji was served by Bhai Dhana Jatt and the village sangat during the ninth Guru’s tour of Malwa.

Gurdwara Sri Gurusar Sahib Patshahi Chhevin
Mehal Kalan  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Hargobind Sahib Ji

Gurdwara Sri Gurusar Sahib at Mehal Kalan remembers the visit of the sixth Guru, Sri Guru Hargobind Sahib Ji, and the seva of Karaha Mal.

Gurdwara Sri Gurusar Sahib Patshahi Nauvin
Sekha  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji

Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji halted at Sekha for two days in January 1666, where the humble seva of Bhai Durgu was honoured above the pride of the powerful.

Gurdwara Sri Kacha Gurusar Sahib
Handiaya  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji

At Handiaya, Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji set aside the prejudice of caste and bathed in the village pond himself, and the sick of the village were healed.

Gurdwara Sri Pakka Gurusar Sahib
Handiaya  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji

The second of the two historic shrines at Handiaya, marking Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji’s stay in the town.

Gurdwara Sri Patshahi Nauvin
Dhilwan  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji

One of two historic shrines at Dhilwan in the Barnala district, commemorating the visit of the ninth Guru.

Gurdwara Sri Sapnisar Sahib Patshahi Dasvin
Bhadaur  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji

Gurdwara Sri Sapnisar Sahib at Bhadaur marks the coming of Guru Gobind Singh Ji in December 1704, when this country was still uninhabited jungle.

Gurdwara Sri Sohiana Sahib Patshahi Nauvin
Sohiana  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji

Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji tied his horse to a Karir tree here in 1665; the tree is preserved at the Gurdwara to this day.

Gurdwara Sri Bairooni Sahib Patshahi Chhevin
Bhadaur  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Hargobind Sahib Ji and Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji

The outer shrine at Bhadaur, honouring both the sixth Guru and the tenth, who came to this place in December 1704.

Gurdwara Sri Jandsar Sahib
Thuliwal  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji

Gurdwara Sri Jandsar Sahib at Thuliwal takes its name from the Jand tree, under which the ninth Guru is remembered to have rested.

Gurdwara Sri Patshahi Nauvin Sahib
Pharwahi  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji

At Pharwahi in 1665, Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji promised the villagers that a teacher would rise from among themselves.

Gurdwara Sri Baba Gurditta Ji Sahib
Daraj  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Baba Gurditta Ji

A shrine at Daraj honouring Baba Gurditta Ji, the eldest son of Guru Hargobind Sahib Ji.

Gurdwara Sri Dulamsar Sahib
Mauran  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji

Gurdwara Sri Dulamsar Sahib at Mauran keeps the memory of the ninth Guru’s passage through this part of Malwa.

Gurdwara Sri Patshahi Satvin Sahib
Gehal  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Har Rai Sahib Ji

Sri Guru Har Rai Sahib Ji passed through Gehal with two thousand two hundred horsemen on the way to Mehraj.

Gurdwara Sri Kailon Sahib Patshahi Nauvin
Dhilwan  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji

The second historic shrine at Dhilwan, commemorating the visit of Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji.

Gurdwara Sri Patshahi Nauvin
Pandher  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji

At Pandher, a village’s discourtesy was answered with forgiveness, and the sangat learned that Guru Sahib does not look at the faults of others.

Gurdwara Sri Patshahi Chhevin Sahib
Kharak Singhwala  ·  Barnala, Punjab  ·  Sri Guru Hargobind Sahib Ji

A shrine at Kharak Singhwala commemorating the visit of the sixth Guru, Sri Guru Hargobind Sahib Ji.
