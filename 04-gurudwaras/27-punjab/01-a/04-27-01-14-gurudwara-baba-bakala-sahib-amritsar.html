Gurudwara Baba Bakala Sahib Ji - Amritsar

In 1664, Guru Har Krishan Ji declared “Baba Bakale” before passing, indicating his successor resided in Bakala


Multiple claimants posed as potential Gurus in the village


• A trader, Baba Makhan Shah Lubana, tested them by offering 2 dinars instead of the vowed 500, and only Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji responded with the phrase, “God bless you... why only 2 dinars after pledging 500? A Sikh is expected to keep his pledge to the Guru.”
This confirmed his authenticity and led Makhan Shah to shout, “Guru Ladho Re!” (“Found the Guru”) from the rooftop

• Guru Tegh Bahadur meditated in concealment underground (in a "bhora") for about 26 years, 9 months, and 13 days before being discovered


The main complex includes four prominent Gurdwaras, each with unique significance:
1. Gurdwara Bhora Sahib o A tall nine-storey structure built above the underground chamber (bhora) where Guru Ji meditated
2. Gurdwara Darbar Sahib o Site where the Sikh Sangat formally performed the Tilak ceremony to anoint Guru Tegh Bahadur as the ninth Guru, led by Diwan Durga Mal and Baba Gurditta
3. Gurdwara Manji Sahib o Location where, during a discourse, a bullet from a Shia masand scraped Guru Ji's turban without harm—symbolizing divine protection
4. Gurdwara Seesh Mahal o Memorial for Mata Ganga Ji, grandmother of Guru Tegh Bahadur and wife of Guru Hargobind Sahib, who passed away here

Address: • Situated in Baba Bakala village, Amritsar district, Punjab, India, along Gurdaspur Road, approximately 42 km east of Amritsar and 46 km from Jalandhar

Easily accessible by road, rail (Butari station ~3 km away), and near major towns
