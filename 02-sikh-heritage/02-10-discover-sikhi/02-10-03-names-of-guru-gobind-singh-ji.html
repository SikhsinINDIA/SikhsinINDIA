Names of Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji Maharaj

Our beloved Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji Maharaj is known by many names and honorific titles including:

• Dasve-patsha (Guru Sahib is the 10th Guru)

• Sarbans Dani (the merciful donor, who sacrificed all)

• Mard Agamra (man without any parallels)

• Baja vala (Guru Sahib kept a hawk)

• Neelay ghoray they swaar (the rider of the blue horse)

• Shah-e-Shahenshah (emperor of emperors)

• Bar do Alam Shah (ruler of both worlds)

• Neelay ghoray whalla (rider of the blue horse)

• Kalgidhar (Guru Sahib wore a Kalgi in his Dastaar)

• Chittay baaja wala (Keeper of the White Falcon)

• Ezzad E Mansoor- Assistance Personified

• Haq Na Ganjur- Keeper of Treasure (Wisdom)

• Jumla Faiz-O-Noor- Giver of Light

• Haq Haq Aagah- Knows the way of god

• Bar Doh Alam Shah- Shah of both worlds

• Dusht-Daman- Destroyer of Devils

• Gobind Rai- His Birth Name

• Gujari Lal- Mata Gujati's son

• Guru Gobind Singh - His official Name

• Dasven/Dashmesh Padshah- Tenth Emperor

• Nanak Dasven- Tenth Nanak

• Mahalla Das- Tenth Form

• Sarbloh Sipahi- Warrior in all Steel

• Padshah Darvesh- Mystic Emperor

• Kaldighar Padshah- Emperor who wears the Kalgi

• Surmaeevale Patshah- The Emperor in Navy

• Shah-I-Shenshah- Shah of all Shahs

• Foujaan Baajaan Satguru- The Guru who wields the army

• Panth De Vaali- Belongs to the Panth

• Dasven Pita- Tenth Father

• Amrit De Daate- Giver of Amrit

• Muqbal-O-Maqbool- Favorite of God
