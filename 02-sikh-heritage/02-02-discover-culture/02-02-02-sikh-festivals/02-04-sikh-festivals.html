Lohri

Lohri for Sikhs is a cultural festival, its themes of community, gratitude, and justice resonate well with Sikh values[cite: 11].

Lohri - The Festival of Harvest

Vaisakhi

Marks the founding of the Khalsa in 1699[cite: 11].

Maghi

Maghi is one of the three festivals chosen by Guru Amar Das Ji to be celebrated by Sikhs (the others being Vaisakhi and Diwali)[cite: 11].

Maghi - The Festival of Liberation

Maghi and the 40 Mukte

Sikhs observe Maghi to honour the heroic fight of the Chali Mukte (Forty Liberated Ones)[cite: 11].

Muktsar Mela

A large fair is held on Maghi in Muktsar commemorating the battle site[cite: 11].

Parkash Utsav Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji

Celebrates the birth of the tenth Sikh Guru[cite: 11].

Holla Mohalla

Started by Guru Gobind Singh Ji at Anandpur Sahib[cite: 11].

Bandi Chhor Divas

Celebrates the release of Guru Hargobind Ji and 52 kings[cite: 11].
