CHAUR SAHIB JI
A Symbol of Honour, Respect & Devotion

Meaning & Significance

Chaur Sahib Ji is a sacred whisk used in Sikh Gurdwaras as a mark of deep respect towards Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji. It symbolizes humility, devotion, and the highest form of seva (selfless service).

HONOUR

Represents deep respect for Guru Granth Sahib Ji.

SEVA

Symbol of selfless service and humility.

ROYALTY

Signifies divine sovereignty of the Guru.

“Seva of the Guru is the highest honour. Waheguru Ji Ka Khalsa, Waheguru Ji Ki Fateh.”
