Sikh Festivals

Chet Sangrand - Sikh New Year
Lohri - The Festival of Harvest

Maghi

Maghi is one of the three festivals chosen by Guru Amar Das Ji to be celebrated by Sikhs (the others being Vaisakhi and Diwali).

Maghi and the 40 Mukte

Sikhs observe Maghi to honour the heroic fight of the Chali Mukte (Forty Liberated Ones).

Muktsar Mela

A large fair is held on Maghi in Muktsar commemorating the battle site.

Parkash Utsav Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji

Celebrates the birth of the tenth Sikh Guru.

Holla Mohalla

Started by Guru Gobind Singh Ji at Anandpur Sahib.

Vaisakhi

Marks the founding of the Khalsa in 1699.

Bandi Chhor Divas

Celebrates the release of Guru Hargobind Ji and 52 kings.
