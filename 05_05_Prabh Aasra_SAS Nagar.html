Prabh Aasra ( Home For Helpless), SAS Nagar

About Us

Prabh Aasra (Universal Disabled Caretaker Social Welfare Society) is a beacon of hope for forgotten souls who are deprived of rehabilitation opportunities and end up on the streets due to mental and physical health issues. For nearly two decades, we have been providing round-the-clock care and treatment by professionals to those who are orphaned, destitute, and unclaimed.

Vision

  • With the Grace of Almighty and support of your all, our vision is to be a source of hope and healing for the most vulnerable and neglected members of society. We envision a world where everyone is treated with dignity and respect, and where everyone has access to the basic necessities of life. We aim to empower our beneficiaries with the skills and opportunities they need to lead fulfilling and productive lives. We believe that every human being deserves compassion and empathy, and that every life can be transformed by love and care. We are committed to promoting ethical values, combating societal ills, and providing timely disaster relief camps. In our quest to create a more compassionate society, we will establish helplines dedicated to assisting the helpless and the needy, with specially equipped ambulances ready to respond to any emergency or accident. Furthermore, we will endeavour to construct an independent city, tailored to the unique needs of individuals with disabilities, offering them a comprehensive range of services and facilities that foster their well-being and independence. Our vision extends to the establishment of a state-of-the-art mental hospital, a specialised school, and a dedicated de-addiction center, all designed to provide comprehensive support to those in need. Together, we will forge a new path forward, where compassion reigns supreme and no individual is left behind. Join us in this noble endeavour as we strive to create a world that embraces the inherent worth and potential of every human life.

Mission Statement

  • Empowering vulnerable individuals to lead dignified lives.

Contributions

  • With the Grace of God, our team of experts provide a safe and nurturing environment for these individuals to heal and recover, empowering them to become respectful and responsible members of society once again. Prabh Aasra is a ray of hope for those who have lost their way, and we are proud to be a part of their journey towards a brighter future.
