GURU ARJAN DEV JI
Divine Attribute: Self-Sacrifice · Guru from 1581 to 1606
The First Great Martyr

Compilation of Adi Granth and Martyrdom

He was born in 1563. He compiled the Adi Granth, the scriptures of the Sikhs, and wrote the Sukhmani Sahib. To make it a universal teaching, Guru ji included in it hymns of Muslim saints as well as those of low-caste pariah saints.

Guru Arjan Dev completed construction of Sri Darbar Sahib — the Golden Temple — in Amritsar. Guru ji became the first great martyr in Sikh history when Emperor Jahangir ordered his execution.
