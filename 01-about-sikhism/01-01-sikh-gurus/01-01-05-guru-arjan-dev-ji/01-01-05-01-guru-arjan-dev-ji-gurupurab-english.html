Commemorating Sri Guru Arjan Dev Ji Gurupurab

Sri Guru Arjan Dev Ji (1563–1606) was the Fifth Sikh Guru. He constructed Harmandir Sahib (The Golden Temple) in Amritsar, compiled the sacred Adi Granth (now Guru Granth Sahib Ji), and composed Sukhmani Sahib. He became the first martyr in Sikh history, demonstrating supreme devotion and submission to the Divine Will (Bhana).
