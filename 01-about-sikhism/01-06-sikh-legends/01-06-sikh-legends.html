Chronicles of Valor, Faith & Sacrifice
Shaheedee  ·  Devotion  ·  Sovereignty  ·  Seva

Explore the lives of historic Sikh warriors, leaders, and martyrs who stood as pillars of righteousness, defended the oppressed, and lived out the divine teachings of the Guru Granth Sahib Ji.

Shaheed Baba Deep Singh Ji

One of the most revered martyrs in Sikh history, famed for his unmatched devotion and heroic defense of Sri Harmandir Sahib.
Maharaja Ranjit Singh Ji

The legendary founder and Emperor of the sovereign Sikh Empire, known as Sher-e-Punjab (The Lion of Punjab).
Baba Banda Singh Bahadur Ji

The fearless military commander who established the first sovereign Sikh rule, striking down tyranny and implementing land reforms.
Hari Singh Nalwa Ji

The legendary Commander-in-Chief of the Sikh Khalsa Army, renowned for expanding boundaries and subduing untamed frontiers.
Sardar Sham Singh Attariwala Ji

The brave general of the Sikh Empire whose legendary last stand at the Battle of Sobraon remains an immortal chapter of courage.
Muktas of Muktsar: Mai Bhago Ji

The heroic warrior saint who led the forty liberated ones (Chali Mukte) back to the battlefield to shield Guru Gobind Singh Ji with supreme devotion and bravery.
Akali Phula Singh Ji

The legendary Nihang commander and Jathedar of Sri Akal Takhat Sahib, known for his unyielding righteousness and martial valor.
Bhai Mani Singh Ji

A premier scholar, scribe of Guru Granth Sahib Ji, and faithful companion of the Gurus who attained supreme martyrdom piece by piece.
Baba Gurbaksh Singh Ji

The revered Nihang warrior who led a tiny band of warriors to protect the sanctity of Sri Harmandir Sahib against massive odds.
Bhai Taru Singh Ji

An absolute epitome of endurance who sacrificed his life and scalp rather than forsake his long hair and faith.
Bhai Jaita Singh Ji (Bhai Jiwan Singh Ji)

The courageous devotee honored as 'Rangreta Guru Ka Beta' for retrieving the sacred head of Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji from Delhi.
