Guru Harkrishan Ji Brahman Te Chhaju
Stories of Guru Harkrishan Ji
A Story of Wisdom and Humility

1. The Learned Brahmin

Once, Guru Harkrishan Ji was staying in Delhi[cite: 15]. A proud Brahmin scholar came to test the Guru's knowledge[cite: 15]. He believed that only those who studied scriptures could understand true wisdom[cite: 15].

2. The Guru’s Response

Guru Harkrishan Ji, still a child, smiled and said, “Even a simple man can explain the meanings of sacred texts if blessed by Waheguru.”[cite: 15] The Brahmin laughed and challenged this idea[cite: 15].

3. Enter Chhaju

Guru Ji called for a simple waterman named Chhaju, who was uneducated and humble[cite: 15]. Guru Ji placed his hand on Chhaju’s head and asked him to explain the verses the Brahmin had chosen[cite: 15].

4. Divine Wisdom Flows

To everyone’s surprise, Chhaju explained the scriptures beautifully[cite: 15]. The Brahmin was shocked[cite: 15]. He bowed his head in shame and then with respect[cite: 15]. He realized true wisdom comes from divine grace, not pride[cite: 15].

💡 The Message

  • 🔶 Knowledge without humility is empty[cite: 15].
  • 🟠 Waheguru can bless anyone, regardless of caste or education[cite: 15].
  • 🟡 Never judge others by appearance or background[cite: 15].

📚 Moral of the Story

“Guru Harkrishan Ji taught that true wisdom comes from Waheguru, not pride or status.”[cite: 15]
