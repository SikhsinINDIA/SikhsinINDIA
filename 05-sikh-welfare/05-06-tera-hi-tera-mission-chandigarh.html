About Us

Tera Hi Tera Mission is a dedicated sewa project of Guru Ka Langer Eye Hospital. We provide essential healthcare services through a comprehensive setup featuring a multi-speciality OPD, pharmacy/chemist, digital X-Ray, CT-Scan, ultrasound, MRI, echocardiogram, and a computerized laboratory.

Mission Statement

  • Tera Hi Tera Mission Hospital is committed to providing medicines at a highly affordable rate of just 13 Rupees!
  • We offer diagnostic services such as ultrasound, MRI, and other medical examinations at very nominal and accessible prices, enabling people to focus fully on their health rather than the burden of financial constraints.
