About Us

Prabh Aasra (Universal Disabled Caretaker Social Welfare Society) is a beacon of hope for forgotten souls who are deprived of rehabilitation opportunities and end up on the streets due to mental and physical health issues.[cite: 7] For nearly two decades, we have been providing round-the-clock care and treatment by professionals to those who are orphaned, destitute, and unclaimed.[cite: 7]

Vision

  • With the Grace of Almighty and support of everyone, our vision is to be a source of hope and healing for the most vulnerable and neglected members of society.[cite: 7] We envision a world where everyone is treated with dignity and respect, and where everyone has access to the basic necessities of life.[cite: 7]
  • We aim to empower our beneficiaries with the skills and opportunities they need to lead fulfilling and productive lives, believing that every life can be transformed by love, care, compassion, and empathy.[cite: 7]
  • We are committed to promoting ethical values, combating societal ills, providing timely disaster relief camps, and establishing dedicated helplines along with specially equipped ambulances ready to respond to any emergency or accident.[cite: 7]
  • Furthermore, we endeavour to construct an independent city tailored to the unique needs of individuals with disabilities, offering a comprehensive range of services and facilities that foster well-being and independence.[cite: 7]
  • Our vision extends to the establishment of a state-of-the-art mental hospital, a specialised school, and a dedicated de-addiction center designed to provide comprehensive support so that no individual is left behind.[cite: 7]

Mission Statement

  • Empowering vulnerable individuals to lead dignified lives.[cite: 7]

Contributions

  • With the Grace of God, our team of experts provide a safe and nurturing environment for these individuals to heal and recover, empowering them to become respectful and responsible members of society once again.[cite: 7] Prabh Aasra is a ray of hope for those who have lost their way, and we are proud to be a part of their journey towards a brighter future.[cite: 7]
