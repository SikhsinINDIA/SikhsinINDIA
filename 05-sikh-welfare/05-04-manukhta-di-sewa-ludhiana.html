Vision

  • We wish to see a smile on the faces of everyone.[cite: 6] With the turn of the century, the family system has deteriorated significantly—from joint to nuclear, from large to small, and from spiritualistic to materialistic.[cite: 6] The powerful have overshadowed the weak, wretched, and sick, resulting in a major part of the underprivileged population becoming mentally, physically, and emotionally ill.[cite: 6] Over the last 15-20 years, many youth have also fallen prey to drug addiction, leading to distressing sights of helpless individuals left neglected on roadsides, in slums, near temples, or outside hospitals, while some mentally ill individuals are even chained up by their own families.[cite: 6]

Mission Statement

  • We aim to structure a better society free from superstition, where people readily carry out their social and family responsibilities, ensuring education, medication, and space for everyone.[cite: 6] We actively search for those who need physical, emotional, or financial aid.[cite: 6] Destitute or impoverished individuals who are sick are treated by dedicated teams from reputed hospitals, and we strive to offer them a homelike environment, medical treatment, counseling, massage, and physiotherapy.[cite: 6] Our ultimate goal is to rehabilitate them as normal human beings living happily with their loved ones, driven by the pure teachings of our Gurus to see everyone happy and smiling.[cite: 6]

Contributions

  • As human beings, we should step forward collectively to support distressed, helpless, and sick people to construct a strong, compassionate society.[cite: 6] Service to humanity stands as the greatest service.[cite: 6]
